Direct purchases can be refunded within 30 calendar days of purchase.

A direct refund requires the order number and original payment receipt.

Approved direct refunds return to the original payment method in five business days.

Direct subscriptions can be canceled in the billing portal at any time.

Canceling a direct subscription stops renewal but keeps access until the paid term ends.

Direct annual plans are not prorated after the refund window closes.

Direct trial accounts expire after 14 days unless upgraded.

Direct invoices are emailed to the billing administrator on the first day of each month.

Direct customers may update their payment method in Billing Settings.

Disputed direct charges must be reported to billing@example.invalid with the invoice ID.
