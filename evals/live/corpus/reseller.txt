Reseller purchases can be refunded within seven calendar days through the reseller.

Reseller refunds require the reseller invoice rather than a direct purchase receipt.

The reseller controls refund processing time and payment method.

Reseller subscription changes must be requested from the reseller account manager.

Reseller purchases are excluded from the direct 30-day refund policy.

Reseller trial access lasts ten days.

Reseller invoices are issued by the reseller, not by direct billing.

A reseller may assign up to two billing contacts for an account.

Reseller account ownership transfers require written approval from both organizations.

Reseller promotional credits expire after 60 days and cannot be exchanged for cash.
