Severity-one incidents receive an initial human response within one hour during the support coverage period.

Support coverage is Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC, excluding published holidays.

Severity-two incidents have an initial response target of eight business hours.

Response targets describe acknowledgment, not guaranteed resolution time.

Escalations require the existing ticket number and a short business-impact summary.

Customers can review ticket updates in the support portal.

Attach redacted diagnostic logs; never attach passwords or API keys to support tickets.

A support ticket closes after ten business days without a customer response.

Closed support tickets can be reopened within five business days.

Emergency phone support is included only in the assisted operations add-on.
