Query history is retained in browser session memory until the page closes.

Evaluation reports persist in the configured job store until an operator deletes them.

Deleted evaluation reports are removed from the primary store within 24 hours of an operator cleanup.

Daily backups are retained for seven days.

Backup restoration is tested monthly using synthetic documents.

Exported reports may contain answers and evidence passages and must be reviewed before sharing.

The demonstration corpus contains synthetic documents only.

Live environments use operator-managed credentials kept outside browser forms.

Corpus identifiers separate retrieval data but do not establish tenant authorization.

A remote live deployment requires authentication and authorization in addition to TLS.
