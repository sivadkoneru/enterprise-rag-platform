# Contoso Reseller Partner Handbook

This handbook governs Contoso's reseller partners. It is not the Northwind Systems employee
handbook and its numbers deliberately differ from Northwind policy.

## Returns and Refunds

Reseller customers may return goods for a full refund within sixty days of delivery. A receipt is
not required where the reseller can supply the original order reference. Restocking charges do not
apply to reseller returns of any kind.

## Purchasing Authority

Reseller account managers may authorise equipment purchases up to five hundred dollars without a
second signature. Larger commitments pass to the regional partner director.

## Support Targets

Partner-raised incidents receive a first response within eight hours at all severities. There is no
separate priority grading for partner tickets.

## Invoicing

Partner invoices are settled on net thirty day terms from the invoice date.
